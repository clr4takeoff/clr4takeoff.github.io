## 연구
[What Do We Need for an Agentic Society?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.03938)

<br>

## Agentic objects
- Autonomy, Reactivity, Proactiveness, Social ability (Wooldridge, Jennings의 이론)

<br>

## Agentic society
- Distributed everyday objects coordinating for collective perception, judgment, and action.
- Propose three coordination phases
  - Perception: what should be shared, and across what boundaries
  - Judgment: how should observations combine, and conflicts resolve
  - Action: when should the collective intervene and who is accountable

<br>

## 가능한 failure
- False Positive: 잘못된 추론
- Deadlock: 권한 부족으로 인해 자기들끼리 결정하지 못하고 멈춤
- Adversarial: 공격

<br>

## Open questions

### Perception
- Boundary: How extensive should an agentic society be?
- Sensitivity: What threshold justifies collective action?
- Privacy: What information may flow between contexts and who controls it?

### Judgment
- Aggregation: How should observations combine into judgements?
- Conflict: How should disagreement between objects be resolved?
- Quorum: How many objects must agree before the collective acts?
- Integrity: How can societies inspect members and verify observations?

### Action
- Escalation: When should societies act autonomously versus notify humans?
- Accountability: When a collective decision causes harm, who bears responsibility?
